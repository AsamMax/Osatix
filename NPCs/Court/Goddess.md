**Type:** NPC

## Overview

The goddess of the nation is a truly public figure. She holds court. She attends public events. She is known for loving sweets and a good show, not seldomly putting one up herself. But she is also a fake. An actress cast to play the part, while the true Goddess looks for a solution to the prophecy of their beloved nation sinking below the waves. All while being told, that if she fails, if anyone spots the ruse, it will spell the end of the nation and it will be her fault alone.
