# Melusines

Tortles are sentient creatures who are remnants of the power of an ancient god who fell in [[Galapas]]. Most live secluded on the island but some work in the city, often in public in mascot like jobs. For example as drivers for the [[Aquabus]]. 

They are diligent and unaccustomed to human society, so most do not know that they are being belittled and treated as lesser beings. But those who notice may hold a deep grudge against the tall people.
