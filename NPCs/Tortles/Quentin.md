# Quentin

**Type:** NPC  
**Relevance:** Session 1 / Harbor Introduction  
**Status:** Planned

## Overview

Quentin is the friendly tortle greeter at the harbor in [[Marelevo]]. He is often the first tortle people meet after arriving, and he helps direct travelers through the port with an anxious slightly bewildered kindness.

## Notes

- Works the harbor welcome point
- Knows the local paperwork routine better than he should
- Means well, even when the bureaucracy does not
