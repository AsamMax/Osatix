## Traits

***Guarddog*.** When it hits a creature with an opportunity attack, the creature's speed becomes 0 (Sentinel Feat)

_**Pounce.**_ If the Hound moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a claw attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked [prone](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ProneCondition). If the target is [prone](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ProneCondition), the tiger can make one bite attack against it as a bonus action.

## Tactics

Follows its masters instructions to the letter. Is capable of autonomous fighting (does not need to be instructed each turn). If it's Master is defeated it will follow it's protocol to return to a designated home -> It is very difficult to overwrite the setting of it running back to a state controlled location and take ownership of it.

## Lore

These Hounds are used as companions for law enforcers in [[Court of Aecor]], or as Guards in the [[Fortress of Meropide]]. None should be in private hands. However, there are similar, non-weaponized dogs available as mechanical pets.
