### Jungle Drake

_Large Dragon, Neutral Evil_

---

- **Armor Class** 15 (Natural Armor)
- **Hit Points** 104 (11d10 + 44)
- **Speed** 30 ft., fly 50 ft.

---

|   STR   |   DEX   |   CON   |  INT   |   WIS   |   CHA   |
| :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :----: | :-----: | :-----: |
| 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 8 (-1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) |

---

- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Con +6
- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Stealth +5
- **Damage Immunities** Poison
- **Condition Immunities** Paralyzed, Poisoned
- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
- **Languages** Draconic, Interpretive Dance
- **Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

**Forest Passage.** The drake ignores difficult terrain caused by non-magical plants, vines, and undergrowth.

#### Actions

**Multiattack.** The drake makes two attacks: one with its Predatory Bite and one with its Stinger. These attacks must target two different creatures.

**Predatory Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it is **grappled** (escape DC 14). Until this grapple ends, the drake can't bite another target, but it can fly and carry the target with it.

**Stinger.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be **poisoned** for 1 minute.

**Spit Venom (Recharge 5–6).** The drake spits a sticky glob of venom at a point within 50 feet, exploding in a 10-foot-radius sphere. Each creature in that area must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) poison damage and becoming **poisoned** for 1 minute on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

#### Reactions

**Twisting Tail.** When a creature within 10 feet of the drake uses its movement to leave the drake's reach, the drake can make one Stinger attack against it. If the attack hits, the creature's speed is reduced to 0 for the rest of the turn.
