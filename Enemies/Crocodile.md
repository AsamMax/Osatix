### Death-Roll Crocodile

_Large Beast, Unaligned_

---

- **Armor Class** 13 (Natural Armor)
- **Hit Points** 34 (4d10 + 12)
- **Speed** 20 ft., swim 30 ft.

---

|   STR   |   DEX   |   CON   |  INT   |   WIS   |  CHA   |
| :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :----: | :-----: | :----: |
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 2 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 5 (-3) |

---

- **Skills** Stealth +5 (+7 in water)
- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
- **Languages** —
- **Challenge** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

**Hold Breath.** The crocodile can hold its breath for 2 hours.

**Aquatic Ambush.** The crocodile has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made while submerged in water. When attacking from stealth in Water, gains walking speed back.

#### Actions

**Jaws.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is **grappled** (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the crocodile can't bite another target.

**Keep in Mouth.** Keeping a target grappled takes an action.

#### Bonus Actions

**Death Roll.** While submerged in water, the crocodile violently twists a creature it currently has grappled. The grappled target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or take 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage and be Fatigued (+1).

- Condition: Fatigue - Like Exhaustion, but removed after 10 minutes of rest instead of long rest.
