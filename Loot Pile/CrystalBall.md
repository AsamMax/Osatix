### **Scrying Ball** (not final)

_Wondrous item, rare, requires attunement_

As an action, while holding the ball, you can place a Tiny Scrying Eye at a point in space you can touch. The eye stays floating in that exact position. It lasts up to 1 hour, but can be dismissed as a bonus action by shaking the orb.

While the eye exists, you can see through the ball as if it was at the eyes location.

The eye cannot move, but you can dismiss it. You can create only one eye at a time.
