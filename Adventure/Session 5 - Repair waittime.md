# Session 5 - Repair waittime

**Type:** NPC / Location / Event / Plot Hook  
**Relevance:** Session X / Arc Y  
**Status:** Active / Planned / Completed

## Overview

The tortles have started repairs on the Osatix - The crew is informed that it will take some time so they have the opportunity to explore

## Key Details

- Travel over the water (how?) => The dinghi of the Osatix perhaps

- **A)** Go towards the city (where the man has flown)
	- On the water: Encounter a semi-see through, round island (giant jellyfish)
		- It is basically harmless, unless attacked
		- It is also too stupid for coherent conversation (answering in single word ideas of water, current, fish, the sun)
	- While interacting with the jellyfish, a diver approaches them (inspired by freminet), called Perre
	- He lives in the Fleuve Cendre below the city and regularly salvages underwater to sell what he finds on the black market there
	- He has no direct contact to the Consortium Baleinier (but he has heard off them)
		- Knows where their headquarters are (small inlet on the island over there)
		- Knows where their branch office in the Fleuve Cendre is (and how to get into the sewers)
		- Knows where the underwater prison is (Fortress of Magusine)
		- Knows where the court is ([[Opera Crépuscule]])
			- It is the residency of their "star", the goddess [[Goddess|étoile Marime]]

- **B)** Go towards the [[Aecor Research Institute]]
	- Discover the bridge is broken
	- Somewhat ruined landscape, building pieces, there was a big explosion
	- In the hollow of an ampitheater there is a tent setup
		- Researcher camp, headed by Alaine
		- He's pretty young and a little in over his head. He was left here to clean up the mess with little help and hope of success (basically a fall guy)
		- He has to file a report by the end of the next day and has no idea how to gather enough data quickly.
		- Asks the group to try to head into one of the flying squares and take a measurement with a device he gives them
			- Has no idea how to get in
			- Has no idea if it is save
			- Has little to give as a reward (except promising to help with any scientific problem in any way he can)
