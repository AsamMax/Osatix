# Session 9 - Into Erinnyes

GM preparation: modular beats following the escape from [[Opera Crépuscule]]. Numbers are references, not a required order. Everything needed to run the situations is in this file; creature references open the existing or published blocks.

## Starting position and scope

The party has fled into [[Erinnyes Forest]] after Arleccia dissolved and a monster appeared on stage. Etoile ordered their arrest so Sigewinne could question them. Dalinee shot Pimpernel during the confusion. The party owes the [[Consortium Baleinier]] help finding/freeing its patron. They know Dalinee requested a visit to that patron in [[Fortress of Magusine]] and Aussire demanded an official application.

**Prepared additions, not previously established facts:** an opera drain empties into these woods; Arleccia's consciousness survives in its runoff; two local Baleiners are escaping the opera; a patrol member independently offers to record testimony or forward a message. Arleccia's recovery is a unique local magical effect, not a general cure for the nation's disappearances. These additions do not establish the cause of the stage disaster or expose any reserved character's secrets.

**The session question:** Where can the party go, and whom will they trust with what happened? They can investigate, negotiate, hide, return to the opera, or leave the island. No destination is mandatory.

## Running the session

**Format:** Preparation is in English. Blockquotes are optional German read-aloud text or NPC dialogue. Each beat follows Purpose / Prerequisites / Location → Entry → Decision and stakes → GM Notes → Combat → Eventualities.

Open with the escape, then respond to the party's stated destination and precautions. Introduce one detail at a time. The two fleeing figures are the first optional human lead; water comes into view later during travel; guard activity begins as sound or tracks. Do not announce all three as a menu.

Aim for roughly 3-4 hours through 4-6 selected beats, including discussion and perhaps 1-2 fights. All nine are potential beats, not a quota. Give decisions and conversations room; do not prolong an encounter after its question is settled. Beats 2-3 and 4-5 split larger situations into discovery and payoff. Beat 7 is separate from spotting the patrol in beat 6.

### Quick selection

| Beat | Purpose | Natural entry | Main pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Leave the lights behind | Let the party establish its escape plan | Opening, or renewed flight | Fast travel versus concealment |
| 2. People taking the old path | Discover who else is escaping | Movement ahead of the party | Pursue, approach, observe, or bypass |
| 3. An overdue promise | Make the Baleiners obligation negotiable | Contact with their two runners | Useful progress versus another empty promise |
| 4. The warm runoff | Make the opera mystery investigable | A gully across a chosen route | Investigate without damaging the evidence |
| 5. A person in the settling pool | Give the mystery a vulnerable human stake | Following the runoff or its collector | Recover Arleccia and choose her care |
| 6. Boots on the slope | Make the official search tangible | Patrol near the party's route | Concealment, diversion, contact, or force |
| 7. A message without a seal | Offer a local guard's help recording or forwarding testimony | The helpful guard's deliberate opening | A traceable account versus exposure |
| 8. The wood has a boundary | Give the forest its own interests | Deeper travel, shelter, or polluted water | Refuge without further encroachment |
| 9. A way off the island | Resolve the party's chosen immediate objective | Shore or aquabus approach | Transport, witnesses, and scrutiny |

### Beat prerequisites at a glance

**Usual connections:** **2 → 3**, **4 → 5**, **6 → 7**. These are three separate threads, not one required chain. Beats **1, 2, 4, 6, 8, and 9** need no earlier beat. Beat 1 is the intended opening, not a prerequisite for everything else.

The required conditions below matter more than completing a numbered beat. The earlier beat normally establishes those conditions; alternative entry points are listed under Eventualities immediately below. No combat victory is a prerequisite.

| Beat | Usual prerequisite beat | Required condition before starting |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Leave the lights behind** | None | The party is escaping or renewing its flight. |
| **2. People taking the old path** | None | The party can notice Mirelle and Bastien while the pair are still in the woods. |
| **3. An overdue promise** | **2**, as the usual introduction | Contact with at least one willing, surviving Baleiners representative who recognises the papers bargain. Following the pair unseen is not enough by itself. |
| **4. The warm runoff** | None | Access to the opera runoff or its outlet. |
| **5. A person in the settling pool** | **4**, as the usual discovery | The party has located Arleccia's coherent water and can reach it. Pool recovery additionally requires that it has not already been removed. |
| **6. Boots on the slope** | None | An existing search patrol can plausibly reach this location. |
| **7. A message without a seal** | **6**, or the escort encounter in **5** | Luc has been encountered, is alive and willing to talk, and has a credible opportunity for private contact. The party does not have to trust him first. |
| **8. The wood has a boundary** | None | The party reaches the marked grove or its affected roots. Discovering the runoff first is unnecessary. |
| **9. A way off the island** | None | The party reaches a departure point or makes a viable crossing plan. Neither a Baleiners agreement nor returning inside the opera is required. |

### Eventualities - replacing or losing a prerequisite

- **Beat 2 skipped, but the party finds the shelter or landing:** Introduce Mirelle and Bastien there while they are still present, establish recognition, then run beat 3 directly. After they have left, their empty shelter cannot deliver the negotiation; a later faction contact must establish a new opportunity.
- **Beat 2 turns violent:** A ceasefire and a surviving representative willing to negotiate can still unlock beat 3. Dead representatives or an unresolved hostile pursuit do not. Do not replace them immediately with another identical pair.
- **Beat 4 skipped, but the party reaches the pool directly:** Its visible forming hand and costume establish the discovery; run beat 5 without requiring them to backtrack and inspect the drain. Beat 8's water diversion can also lead them toward this location.
- **Arleccia's water diverted elsewhere:** Find and reach its actual new location before using beat 5's recovery procedure. Preserve the changed geography rather than restoring the planned pool.
- **Arleccia already collected:** The pool-recovery version of beat 5 is no longer available there. Tags and tracks can lead to local custody at the opera holding station. Access to the labelled vessel becomes the new prerequisite for attempting recovery; resolve permission, stealth, or confrontation first. Return to the opera remains the players' choice.
- **Beat 6 skipped, but the escort arrives during beat 5:** Luc can be introduced with that same patrol. Establish his individual hesitation and an opportunity to talk before beat 7. Merely hearing that guards exist does not unlock private contact.
- **Luc dead, hostile, or unable to separate from the patrol:** Skip or postpone beat 7. Normal witness and message procedures remain available elsewhere, but do not transfer his personal offer automatically to another guard.
- **Beat 3 declined or bypassed:** Beat 9 remains available through payment, negotiation, public transport, or the party's own resources. Only Mirelle's introduction and its associated credit are unavailable.
- **Beats 4-5 bypassed:** Beat 8 still works: the keeper can point out the affected roots and explain the local diversion without the party having met Arleccia. Repairing the drain earlier satisfies the same task; do not repeat it.

### Local geography

Opera to the east; deeper woods and the Weeping Willow to the west, following the existing forest note. A proposed old service path skirts a runoff gully. Its settling pool is below the opera's drain; an abandoned groundskeeper shelter lies on a dry spur nearby. The public route returns toward the opera's Navia Line aquabus stop. A rough shore landing offers a possible private water crossing. These are additions to the local map, not new inter-island land connections.

Keep travel distances loose: one meaningful stretch of cautious travel is about 10-20 minutes. Establish relative positions and exits before a confrontation. Do not relocate bypassed NPCs ahead of the party merely to force contact.

### People and their limits

| NPC | Wants now | Knows | Does not know |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mirelle**, Baleiners negotiator, new NPC | An actionable update and her people safely out | The papers bargain; a local landing and boat owner | The artifact scheme's true mastermind or how to free the patron |
| **Bastien**, her companion, new NPC | Avoid a patrol and protect Mirelle | Service paths, a sheltered meeting place | What happened beneath the stage |
| **Arleccia** | Become whole; avoid confinement and further immersion | Her last sensations before dissolving | Who caused it, whether the monster was her, or a universal cure |
| **Luc**, patrol guard, new NPC | Prevent needless casualties; preserve an accurate account | Existing arrest order; ordinary witness and message procedures | Any reserved character's plans or private instructions |
| **Sergent Vau**, patrol commander, new NPC | Return with living witnesses and a credible report | Descriptions, theater identities, orders to preserve unusual material | The party's guilt or the forest's magical rules |
| **The boundary keeper**, voice of an awakened tree | Stop trespass and polluted runoff | Local watercourses and recent disturbance | Court politics and the prophecy's solution |

Luc's help is his own initiative. He has no private commission from Dalinee or any other senior figure. Dalinee remains the person who shot at Pimpernel; Luc cannot explain or reinterpret that action.

## Eventualities - the world between beats

Record elapsed fictional time and advance each thread independently. The timings below are starting estimates; travel, messengers, and the party's actions determine actual progress. One short conversation does not advance everything. Announce changing conditions through observable signs.

| Thread | Initial state | After roughly 30-45 minutes unattended | After roughly 90 minutes unattended | By dawn, still unattended |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Official search | One patrol checks the forest edge; opera staff evacuate | Descriptions reach the Navia stop; staff begin securing drains | Collection staff reach the settling pool; public departures require questioning | Witness lists and the troupe identity reach city offices; search shifts to known routes and contacts |
| Baleiners | Mirelle and Bastien head to their sheltered meeting place | They wait briefly, then continue to their landing | They return toward Fleuve Cendre with their report | Branch office demands a specific update and becomes less generous with credit |
| Runoff / Arleccia | Water and fragments collect in the settling pool | Her voice becomes more distinct; drain flow diminishes | Local cleanup staff can bottle the coherent portion and log the costume | Arleccia remains in a labelled vessel at a temporary opera holding station; subsequent transfer is undecided; the pool retains residue and a collection tag |
| Forest boundary | Fae watch the intrusion | Runoff reaches roots; animals avoid the lower gully | The keeper diverts one woodland path away from the affected ground | Fae deny the polluted hollow as a campsite; no automatic forest-wide attack |

- **Engaging a thread:** Update only what the action affects. Blocking the drain changes the water's path; persuading Vau changes that patrol's report, not every official's orders. Reported descriptions take a messenger or signal to spread.
- **Ignoring every lead:** Honour their destination and run beat 9 or a travel resolution. The consequences above remain real elsewhere. Do not have the Baleiners, Arleccia, and Luc all arrive at their camp.
- **Returning straight to the opera:** Use beat 6 at a service entrance; the drain in beat 4 can be found from its upper end. Staff are cleaning and listing witnesses. Entry, disguise, testimony, or retreat remain available; do not force them back into the forest.
- **Heading toward the Research Institute:** Support the choice without routing them through the opera interior. Use beat 9 for a private crossing toward the institute's accessible shore. The [[Aquabus]] Callas Line is already out of order; do not reopen it for convenience. Existing notes establish the ruined institute, a broken bridge, and a temporary camp in an old coliseum headed by the junior researcher Alaine. These provide a destination and later-session handoff, not a mandatory new quest. Local boat owners know the landing and damaged approach, not the cause of the explosion. Record the party's objective for Panu and leave senior staff appearances and new research revelations open.
- **Seeking Lotte:** No automatic rescue. A message can go through a returning opera worker or a known city contact. A successful delivery produces a later meeting request; a failed or intercepted delivery exposes the rendezvous, not Lotte's inexplicable presence here.
- **Going directly to the ship:** They still need a water crossing from the opera island. Resolve the shore approach in beat 9, then let them head toward the Osatix's last established location. Verify its repair status at the table; do not reset it or invent a new confiscation deadline.
- **Resting:** A concealed, dry site can support a short rest. Resolve its security once using their precautions, rather than rolling interruptions repeatedly. Advance the relevant offscreen threads for the hour. A long rest advances events to dawn; it does not automatically fail or solve the investigation.
- **Splitting the party:** Follow their actual locations. Keep encounters small; use the three-PC column as a ceiling, not a suitable fight for one isolated PC. Give warning and exits before initiative.
- **Capture:** Continue play with a guarded holding room, named witnesses, and local custody procedures. Confiscated evidence has a destination and receipt. Senior interviews, new orders, or transfers require Panu's continuation; do not invent competing requests from reserved NPCs. Capture does not automatically reveal secrets, free the patron, or end the campaign.

## Beat 1 - Leave the lights behind

**Purpose:** Establish the party's immediate objective and let escape precautions change the search.

**Prerequisites:** None. Intended opening; may also be reused for renewed flight.

**Location:** Forest edge west of the opera; a wet gully below a dry ridge.

### Entry

**Trigger:** Start during the escape, or when flight resumes.

> Zwischen den Stämmen blitzen noch die Lichter der Oper auf. Hinter euch vermischen sich Rufe mit dem Rauschen der Blätter. Vor euch fällt der nasse Boden zu einer Rinne ab; daneben zieht sich ein trockenerer Rücken durch den Wald.

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** Keep running along easy ground, move slowly under cover, stop to assess injuries, or establish a destination. Ask what each PC is doing during the escape. Let their methods determine what information they gain.

**Stakes:** Time, a traceable trail, and access to a secure pause. They are not surrounded at the start.

### GM Notes

- **Information:** Describe ridge and gully freely; use the actual marching order and lighting. The party is not surrounded.
- **Check:** One DC 13 Survival or Stealth check for an uncertain navigation or concealment plan. Suitable magic or concrete precautions can make a roll unnecessary.
- **Pacing:** Ask what each PC does while escaping, then let their destination and methods shape the next lead.

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** 1 [Veteran](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17045-veteran), 1 [Mechanical Hound](</D:/dev/Osatix/Enemies/Automatons/Mechanical Hound.md>), 1 [Guard](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters#Guard) (+1 Guard); an isolated scout ahead of the patrol uses Guard alone.

### Eventualities

- **Engage:** Scouting reveals a useful local landmark and makes the next lead easier to evaluate. Pausing allows treatment and a real discussion of where to go. Introduce beat 2 during renewed movement, not during their first sentence.
- **Bypass / keep sprinting:** They gain distance quickly but leave an easier trail. The search can later reach their last stopping point; it still needs to follow evidence. Proceed toward their chosen destination.
- **Provoke combat:** Deliberately lure pursuers close and attack them: use the patrol listed in this beat. They can instead ambush an isolated forward Guard for a much easier encounter; do not add the full patrol without warning.
- **Failed route/concealment check:** Lose about ten minutes or leave a visible trace; describe which. Do not introduce an instant melee.
- **Likely consequences:** A clean trail break delays this patrol's approach by one travel interval. A loud fight allows nearby searchers to locate the fighting, not automatically identify everyone involved.

## Beat 2 - People taking the old path

**Purpose:** Offer a human lead before the water mystery or direct guard contact, with enough uncertainty for pursuit and observation to matter.

**Prerequisites:** No earlier beat. Mirelle and Bastien must still be within reach in the woods. Usually leads to **beat 3** after contact and recognition.

**Location:** Old service path: fern-covered descent, exposed clearing, then a fork at a maintenance post.

### Entry

**Trigger:** The party notices two figures crossing its route while they are still in the woods.

> Weiter vorn huschen zwei Gestalten über eine Lücke zwischen den Bäumen. Eine blickt in eure Richtung und raunt der anderen etwas zu. Dann verschwinden beide auf einem schmalen, überwucherten Weg.

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** Follow unseen, approach openly, get ahead of them, or leave them alone. Mirelle and Bastien believe someone might be following them from the opera. They want to identify the follower before using their landing.

**Stakes:** Losing the pair, exposing the party's own trail, or earning a calmer first conversation.

### GM Notes

- **Pursuit details:** Use the fern descent, exposed clearing, and marked fork as changing terrain, not three mandatory rolls.
- **Observable behaviour:** One figure checks behind; the other studies the ground. Their ordinary bootprints differ from the patrol's disciplined line.
- **Checks:** DC 13 Survival to recover a lost trail; use the Scout's senses for stealth contests. Calling out or a clever interception can settle the approach directly.

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** Mirelle uses [Bandit Captain](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16799-bandit-captain); Bastien uses [Scout](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters#Scout). Both seek escape.

### Eventualities

- **Engage:** Following or hailing them reaches recognition and beat 3. Careful observation first reveals that they avoid guard lanterns and carry a familiar paperwork wallet. The pair ask who sent the party before revealing their affiliation.
- **Bypass:** The pair go to their meeting shelter and then landing on the stated timeline. They do not chase the party. A later report to the branch office says the party was seen fleeing the opera, with no update on its promise.
- **Lose them:** The party can spend ten minutes reacquiring the trail or move on. Tracks lead to the shelter; there is no mandatory successful roll to preserve access to the faction.
- **Provoke combat:** Attacking or physically trapping the pair starts a fight with the pair. Their objective is escape. Mirelle offers a ceasefire on recognition; Bastien covers withdrawal. Killing them has faction consequences, not a fresh wave of invisible reinforcements.
- **Likely consequences:** A respectful approach earns a private conversation. Threatening them makes beat 3 begin with drawn weapons and a demand for distance.

## Beat 3 - An overdue promise

**Purpose:** Turn the neglected patron bargain into an active negotiation with several acceptable outcomes.

**Prerequisites:** Contact and recognition with a willing, surviving Baleiners representative; **beat 2** normally establishes this. Direct contact at the shelter or landing can replace beat 2; see the prerequisite Eventualities above.

**Location:** Abandoned groundskeeper's shelter on a dry spur beside the old service path, near the runoff gully. Door and rear slope are separate exits.

### Entry

**Trigger:** The party makes contact with the pair, normally at their shelter after beat 2.

> Auf einem trockenen Vorsprung steht eine verlassene Hütte. Drinnen steht eine abgedunkelte Laterne auf einer Kiste. Zwischen zwei Holzbänken liegt eine kleine Karte ausgebreitet; hinter der Hütte fällt der Hang zum Wald ab.

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** Mirelle wants a usable next step on the patron bargain; the party wants shelter, transport, information, or relief from pressure. What information or commitment will they trade?

**Stakes:** Continued faction credit, a possible private crossing, and what information the party gives away.

### GM Notes

**Mirelle's opening:**

> „Unsere Papiere haben euch in die Stadt gebracht. Was habt ihr für unseren Patron getan?“

- **What she knows:** The original bargain, not the party's private conversations. Let the players explain their progress.
- **Useful information:** Dalinee's attempted visit and Aussire's insistence on formal channels are new to her.
- **What she offers:** A short-rest site, a boat introduction, or delivery of a message to the branch office. She cannot grant prison access or cancel warrants.
- **Her proposed deal:** One useful lead now, a named next step, and a check-in by the following evening. She can attempt to send a message to the patron through existing contacts.
- **Checks:** Persuasion DC 13 improves a plausible bargain; Insight DC 12 reveals frustration rather than instructions to punish them.

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** Same [Bandit Captain](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16799-bandit-captain) and [Scout](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters#Scout) from beat 2; no fresh reinforcements.

### Eventualities

- **Engage honestly:** Concrete opera intelligence restores provisional goodwill. The pair supply their local map and a boat introduction; mark what information they will report home. The promise is renewed, not magically fulfilled.
- **Engage with deception:** A plausible account buys time. Record the claim and who can verify it; discovery later costs trust. Do not use mind reading from the branch peacekeeper at a distance.
- **Refuse / bypass:** Mirelle lets them walk. She reports the absence of progress and withholds further unsecured favours. Existing papers do not vanish, and the faction does not automatically betray them to the guards.
- **Provoke combat:** Assault, theft of the map/wallet, or trying to hold either NPC hostage starts a fight with the pair. They seek disengagement, not a duel to the death. Destruction of their shelter makes future aid a reparations negotiation.
- **Likely consequences:** An agreement gives a practical route toward beat 9 or a rest. Refusal leaves those routes available through the party's own resources. No faction lead is required to find the runoff.

## Beat 4 - The warm runoff

**Purpose:** Let curiosity connect the stage disaster to the forest without revealing Arleccia immediately.

**Prerequisites:** No earlier beat; access to the runoff or outlet. Usually leads to **beat 5** through investigation, but can stand alone.

**Location:** Opera runoff gully, beside the stone outlet and the grate dividing the natural bed from the settling channel.

### Entry

**Trigger:** The party crosses the gully, uses it to obscure tracks, or approaches the outlet.

> Ein schmales Rinnsal kreuzt euren Weg. Im Wasser treiben Ruß, Glitzer und bunte Papierfetzen. Es kommt aus Richtung der Oper. Weiter oben erkennt ihr zwischen den Wurzeln einen gemauerten Auslass.

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** Cross and leave, investigate its source, or follow an anomaly downstream. A partly jammed grate splits the current between a natural bed and a settling pool.

**Stakes:** Time, preserving material that may be a person, and the path of polluted runoff into fae territory.

### GM Notes

- **Free clues:** Stage debris and visible flow need no roll. Touch reveals a warm patch; close watching reveals red thread briefly moving against the current; sustained listening reveals a broken rhythm resembling a performer's count.
- **Interpretation:** Investigation or Arcana DC 13 identifies a coherent patch moving separately from the ordinary current.
- **Mechanism:** A visible maintenance lever shifts the grate; tools or DC 13 Athletics work. Its diagram shows the settling-pool branch.
- **Directions:** Upstream reveals the opera drain and cleanup activity; downstream leads toward beat 5. Neither direction establishes the water's original source or a culprit.

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** 2 [Death-Roll Crocodiles](</D:/dev/Osatix/Enemies/Crocodile.md>) (+1 crocodile); they defend the reedbed and stop at the dry bank.

### Eventualities

- **Engage:** Tracing the coherent patch reaches the settling pool and beat 5. Studying the outlet establishes that something from beneath the stage reached this watercourse. It does not prove who put it there.
- **Bypass:** The water continues toward the pool and forest roots. Arleccia remains there until the collection team arrives on the timeline. Her later absence leaves physical evidence and a custody trail.
- **Divert or dam the water:** Read the actual channels. A diversion toward the settling pool concentrates the coherent patch and helps recovery. Sending the entire flow elsewhere moves her with it and changes which roots are affected; she does not disappear because the planned pool was skipped.
- **Provoke combat:** Wading into the reedbed and attacking or cornering its crocodiles provokes the crocodiles. Warning: a broad wake, floating eyes, and gnawed fish. They defend the reedbed; retreat to the dry bank ends pursuit. Merely examining the drain does not trigger an attack.
- **Failed interpretation check:** Reveal the anomaly anyway, at the cost of time or an audible splash while examining the grate.
- **Likely consequences:** Careful diversion reduces fae hostility in beat 8. Burning or indiscriminately poisoning the water damages evidence and adds a concrete grievance; it is not an automatic instant death for Arleccia.

## Beat 5 - A person in the settling pool

**Purpose:** Make recovering Arleccia a playable task, then put her wishes at the centre of the decision about her care.

**Prerequisites:** Locate and reach Arleccia's coherent water; **beat 4** normally establishes this. Direct pool discovery or following a diversion can replace beat 4. Collection changes the required location and access; see the prerequisite Eventualities above.

**Location:** Settling pool below the opera drain; a low stone lip and an outlet sluice control the water.

### Entry

**Trigger:** The party reaches the coherent water, normally by following beat 4 downstream.

> Hinter einer niedrigen Steinkante sammelt sich das Wasser. Ein rotes Stück Stoff kreist darin. Plötzlich formt sich eine Hand, greift nach dem Stoff und zerfließt wieder. Zwischen den Blasen hört ihr eine Stimme: „Mein Einsatz … ich habe ihn verpasst …“

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** Keep her coherent long enough to recover her, choose what to risk or use, and decide where to take a frightened witness. The outflow is pulling the coherent patch apart.

**Stakes:** Arleccia's immediate safety, access to her testimony, and whether she becomes a protected person or officially collected evidence.

### GM Notes

**Recovery procedure — session-specific magic**

Explain the three visible problems before asking for actions. No single spell is required.

| Task | Practical approach | Check when uncertain |
|---|---|---|
| **Contain** | Close the outlet sluice, build a barrier, or gather the coherent patch in a clean broad container. A small flask preserves only a speaking fragment. | Athletics DC 13 for brute force; tools and sound engineering can suffice. |
| **Anchor** | Address Arleccia by name, touch the costume to the water, or repeat her opening count. Her response provides feedback. | Performance or Persuasion DC 13; a directly appropriate action needs no roll. |
| **Sustain** | Keep the anchored water still and help her reform; another PC can assist or manage the sluice. | Arcana, Medicine, or Performance DC 14; appropriate magic can provide support. |

- **Timing:** A substantial attempt takes a few minutes outside combat; each task takes an action during initiative. Containment stops dispersal. A brief pause is not a death timer.
- **Scope:** Healing can contribute to this local effect without becoming a universal cure. Use changing problems and feedback rather than three identical rolls.
- **After recovery:** She needs clothing, warmth, and reassurance. She wants a familiar troupe member or Lotte and fears being sealed in a vessel.

**Arleccia's testimony**

- **Reliable memories:** Taking her opening position, feeling water, then losing any clear sense of her body.
- **Uncertain:** The order of the mechanism, her transformation, and the monster's appearance. She cannot confirm sabotage, a culprit, whether the monster was her, or any reserved NPC's role.
**Possible words:**

> „Ich stand auf meinem Platz. Dann war da Wasser. Danach wusste ich nicht mehr, wo mein Körper aufhört.“

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** Collection escort: 1 [Veteran](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17045-veteran), 1 [Mechanical Hound](</D:/dev/Osatix/Enemies/Automatons/Mechanical Hound.md>), 1 [Guard](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters#Guard) (+1 Guard); workers flee.

### Eventualities

- **Engage:** Successful recovery gives an autonomous, grateful witness, not a combat ally. Transporting the contained patch preserves access to her voice but requires continued care. She chooses whether to accompany the party once able to decide.
- **Bypass / abandon:** Local cleanup staff arrive on the world timeline and log the coherent water, costume, time, and destination: a temporary holding station at the opera's service yard. Leave a numbered collection tag and workers' tracks. They preserve unusual material as part of securing the damaged building, without a new personal order from Sigewinne. Arleccia remains recoverable there. Any onward transfer or research decision is left open for Panu.
- **Hand her to officials:** Vau can promise a named witness entry and a receipt, not freedom or confidentiality. Arleccia protests confinement. Her account remains available through an interview request; the party loses control of its distribution.
- **Provoke combat:** Threatening or attacking the arriving collection escort starts a fight with the patrol. Telegraph its approach with lanterns and clinking containers. Use the existing patrol from beat 6, not a second fresh group. Workers retreat and do not fight. A contained Arleccia is not damaged merely because initiative starts; an attacker must actually target her container or disrupt her water.
- **Recovery setback:** A sluice slips, a sentence fades, or cloth drifts toward the outlet; give feedback and a chance to correct it. After three uncorrected setbacks, Arleccia remains stable only as a contained speaking patch for now. She can still travel and receive help later.
- **Likely consequences:** Recovery supplies a surviving witness and her limited account, without proving a culprit or chronology. Collection puts that witness in local custody first. Loss of a container spills the patch locally and creates an urgent containment problem, not an offscreen disappearance.

## Beat 6 - Boots on the slope

**Purpose:** Put the search in reach and distinguish an individual guard's judgment from the existing arrest order.

**Prerequisites:** No earlier beat; a patrol must plausibly be in reach. Can establish Luc and the opportunity needed for **beat 7**. The escort in beat 5 is this same patrol, not an additional group.

**Location:** A slope, path junction, or service entrance on the patrol's actual search route.

### Entry

**Trigger:** The patrol comes within sight or hearing, or the party studies its fresh tracks.

> Vom Hang her knirschen Schritte im Laub. Ein abgeschirmter Laternenschein streicht zwischen den Stämmen hindurch. Jemand sagt gedämpft: „Abstand halten. Achtet auf die Wege.“

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** Observe, hide, create a diversion, negotiate, surrender, or attack. Vau wants the party alive; Luc wants a chance to talk privately without publicly disobeying the commander.

**Stakes:** Identification, custody, and an opportunity to notice a guard hesitating over the treatment of witnesses.

### GM Notes

- **Search:** Follow the actual trail and the arrest order already given in play. Telegraph the patrol before contact unless the party has made its location obvious.
**Overheard exchange:**

> „Wir haben Beschreibungen, aber keine Aussage dazu, was passiert ist.“
>
> „Dann bringen wir sie rein und nehmen eine auf. Waffen unten lassen.“

- **Luc's action:** He covers a trace and directs Vau toward another track. This is his own hesitation about provoking a fight, not a secret instruction from a superior.
- **Observation and concealment:** Witnessing that action needs no special Insight check. Use actual cover, lighting, and the patrol's senses; a diversion can move the search without a full group Stealth roll.

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** 1 [Veteran](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17045-veteran), 1 [Mechanical Hound](</D:/dev/Osatix/Enemies/Automatons/Mechanical Hound.md>), 1 [Guard](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters#Guard) (+1 Guard); seek living prisoners.

### Eventualities

- **Engage peacefully:** Vau requests identities, weapons kept sheathed, and an account. A credible offer of testimony may arrange an escorted interview; he cannot revoke Etoile's order. Luc tries to create an opening for beat 7.
- **Observe / evade:** Successful evasion preserves freedom. Observing Luc's action offers a choice to signal or leave a message. No contact is forced.
- **Bypass without watching:** The patrol searches its sector and reports its actual findings. Luc does not abandon duty to find the party anywhere on the island. Later contact must come through a message or another plausible encounter.
- **Provoke combat:** Attacking, assaulting a detained worker, or openly forcing through an attempted arrest starts a fight with the patrol. An insult alone does not. Vau calls for surrender; melee knockouts are the preferred capture method. The hound's ranged fire is dangerous and not a nonlethal option; its handler orders melee capture rather than firing into fleeing civilians.
- **Likely consequences:** A peaceful account records the party as witnesses as well as suspects. Visible killings harden the search; concealed escape without casualties leaves room for negotiation. The patrol's whistle brings a warning of later help, not same-round reinforcements.

## Beat 7 - A message without a seal

**Purpose:** Offer a local channel for testimony or a message while leaving the senior NPCs' responses undecided.

**Prerequisites:** Luc has been encountered through **beat 6** or **beat 5's escort**, remains alive and willing to talk, and has an opportunity for private contact. This beat is unavailable until that setup exists; merely bypassing a patrol does not establish it.

**Location:** A junction near the patrol route, briefly out of the other guards' sight.

### Entry

**Trigger:** Luc has a credible moment alone after contact in beat 6 or with beat 5's escort. The patrol returns in roughly ten minutes.

> Die übrigen Schritte entfernen sich. Luc stellt seine Laterne ab und hält die Hände so, dass ihr sie sehen könnt. „Ich habe Leute weglaufen sehen. Wer das angefangen hat, habe ich nicht gesehen. Wenn ihr etwas zu sagen habt, kann ich dafür sorgen, dass es aufgeschrieben wird.“

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** Give an account, ask Luc to forward a message, refuse, or expose him. He offers to record what they say and deliver a sealed note to an ordinary duty desk, addressed to whomever they choose.

**Stakes:** What goes on record, Luc's position, and how much identifying information the party risks disclosing.

### GM Notes

- **Luc's offer:** Record a statement, sign a copy, or carry a sealed note to an ordinary duty desk. He can give the desk's location for checking delivery.
- **Reasonable requests:** A named local witness, a signed copy, separate performer accounts, and written acknowledgement of physical evidence.
- **Limits:** He can bind only his own conduct. He cannot arrange a senior meeting, promise a reply, or speak for a reserved NPC. He knows nothing of the attempted Magusine visit beyond what the party tells him.
- **Handoff:** Record the chosen recipient and exact message. Leave all reserved NPC responses to Panu; questions about their motives stay unanswered.

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** 1 [Guard](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters#Guard); surviving patrol members can arrive after two rounds, within hearing of his whistle.

### Eventualities

- **Engage:** Luc records the account or carries the exact sealed message agreed. He can provide his signed acknowledgement; successful delivery produces a duty-desk log entry, not a guaranteed personal reply. Record delivery status for Panu to resolve the recipient's response later.
- **Refuse / bypass:** Luc rejoins the patrol and files no invented private conversation. The ordinary search and local reporting continue. No reserved NPC's attitude or plan changes automatically because the party ignored Luc.
- **Demand proof:** Luc can show his service identification and the public arrest description. These verify his role, not his trustworthiness or any superior's goodwill. The party may give a limited account and withhold sensitive information.
- **Provoke combat:** Attacking Luc starts with one **Guard**, not a surprise boss. His whistle can call the surviving members of **search patrol** after two full rounds, provided they are within hearing; announce approaching steps. Threats can instead make him withdraw. Do not spawn replacements for dead or distant patrol members.
- **Likely consequences:** The party can establish a traceable account or outgoing message, not immunity. Betraying Luc closes his personal help; normal channels and other routes remain available.

## Beat 8 - The wood has a boundary

**Purpose:** Make Erinnyes an inhabited place with its own interests, and offer refuge independent of the three main leads.

**Prerequisites:** No earlier beat; reach the grove or affected roots. Beats 4-5 are optional context, not requirements. Earlier successful diversion already fulfils the keeper's water-related request.

**Location:** Marked grove deeper in Erinnyes, beside roots affected by the runoff.

### Entry

**Trigger:** The party reaches the boundary while travelling deeper, seeking shelter, or following the affected water.

> Tücher hängen an einem Ast. Darunter markieren sorgfältig aufgeschichtete Steine eine Grenze. Als das Licht über einen alten Stamm wandert, dreht sich darin langsam ein Gesicht zu euch.

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** The keeper demands that the party keep flame off living wood, leave marked paths intact, and stop foul water entering the roots before camping in the sheltered hollow. They can negotiate, repair the diversion, leave, or challenge the boundary.

**Stakes:** A secure short-rest site, continued access through the grove, and whether the fae treat these fugitives as more careless encroachers.

### GM Notes

**The keeper's accusation:**

> „Euer Haus aus Stein spült seine Krankheit in unsere Wurzeln.“

- **Meaning:** The keeper identifies the opera as the source of this runoff, not the party as its cause.
- **Remedy:** Divert the foul flow with earth and stones into the existing settling channel. Labour and a sound plan suffice; use Survival or Athletics DC 13 for an uncertain attempt.
- **Offer:** A dry short-rest hollow and directions to a shore path. The keeper has local knowledge, not answers about the prophecy.
- **Connection:** This water problem can reveal the runoff from another direction; meeting Arleccia is unnecessary.

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** 1 [Awakened Tree](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16792-awakened-tree) (+1 tree); stop pursuit at the boundary stones.

### Eventualities

- **Engage:** Respecting the boundary and stopping the local pollution earns one safe short rest and directions. Advance outside events for the rest's actual duration. Successful prior work on the drain already counts; do not require it twice.
- **Negotiate another service:** A concrete promise to bring tools or remove abandoned metal can buy passage, but shelter beside still-polluted roots remains unavailable. Record the obligation and agreed deadline.
- **Bypass / leave:** The keeper lets them skirt the grove. Travel costs another interval; no pursuit follows respectful refusal. Unattended pollution leads the fae to close that hollow and divert a path on the world timeline.
- **Provoke combat:** Cutting the marked tree, setting the grove alight, or attacking the keeper provokes the boundary trees. It first orders them out, then fights to expel them. Withdrawal beyond the stones ends pursuit. Do not add the nearby guard encounter on top of this fight.
- **Likely consequences:** The forest can become a useful local refuge or a place requiring restitution. Its cooperation does not clear official suspicion or settle the Baleiners debt.

## Beat 9 - A way off the island

**Purpose:** Pay off the party's declared destination and turn what they learned into a concrete next-session position.

**Prerequisites:** No earlier beat; reach a departure point or propose a viable crossing. Beat 3 can supply an introduction, beat 8 directions, and beat 7 a message drop, but none is required for departure.

**Location:** Either the rough shore landing or the public Navia Line stop; use the party's chosen departure point.

### Entry

**Trigger:** The party reaches its chosen landing or station, or proposes its own crossing.

**At the private landing:**

> Zwischen Schilf und flachen Steinen liegt ein schlichtes Boot vertäut. Eine Frau prüft die Leinen und blickt zu euch auf. „Wohin soll es gehen?“

**At the public stop:**

> Am Anleger warten noch Menschen aus der Oper. Jemand mit einer Schreibtafel nimmt Namen auf. Daneben liegt der Aquabus im Wasser.

### Decision and stakes

**Decision:** Choose transport and decide what witnesses, evidence, debts, or official scrutiny to carry with them. At the rough landing, **Neris**, a new local boat owner, wants safe passage for herself and payment for lost work. At the public stop, officials are recording evacuated passengers and may have received the descriptions by now.

**Stakes:** Leaving this island, protecting other people from involvement, and who knows the party's destination.

### GM Notes

- **Private landing:** Neris has a plain boat for the party, ordinary gear, and one passenger. More people need another trip. Her opening price is 20 gp, negotiable through aid, a credible IOU, or Mirelle's introduction.
- **Neris's limits:** She will not knowingly ram a checkpoint; the boat has no magical protection from tracking.
- **Public stop:** Resolve disguise, truthful testimony, ordinary boarding, or voluntary escort against the actual information officials have received.
- **Destinations:** Ask where they want to go before launching. Maintain the island geography and the closed Callas Line; departure need not take them inside the opera.

**Combat (3 PCs; additions for 4 in parentheses):** Landing: 2 [Scouts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters#Scout) (+1 Scout); public stop: surviving patrol from beat 6. Use only the chosen location's opposition.

### Eventualities

- **Engage at the landing:** A fair arrangement gets them across toward an agreed accessible shore. Neris asks which destination before launching. Mirelle's introduction waives the cash price in favour of a recorded faction debt; that is a meaningful choice, not a free rescue.
- **Engage at the public stop:** Before descriptions arrive, ordinary departure is possible with the existing papers. Afterward, questioning can produce a witness statement, delayed boarding, or arrest depending on what they reveal and how they respond. Use surviving **search patrol** personnel for enforcement, never a second unannounced identical patrol.
- **Choose the Research Institute:** Neris can cross toward its accessible shore; she warns of the damaged approach and does not promise docking inside the ruins. The closed Callas Line remains closed. End with the chosen landing or the approach to the temporary camp, leaving any senior involvement and research revelations for Panu. This route requires neither entering the opera nor accepting a faction favour.
- **Bypass / use their own transport:** A functional dinghy, flight, swimming, or another plausible resource can solve the crossing. Resolve capacity, range, and exhaustion using the actual abilities involved. Do not invent a storm to negate a successful plan.
- **Ignore departure / remain in the woods:** End at their chosen shelter and advance the world timeline. By dawn the public route has witness checks and Arleccia may be in custody. The private landing remains a place, although Neris leaves for work at dawn rather than waiting forever.
- **Provoke combat:** Trying to steal Neris's boat brings resistance from Neris and her deckhand, using two **Scouts**; for four PCs a third worker is present. They defend the mooring and offer a surrender once the theft stops. At the public stop use **search patrol**. Choose the relevant opposition, never combine both sites. Retreat leaves the transport dispute unresolved rather than starting a naval battle.
- **Likely consequences:** Record destination, surviving witnesses, Arleccia's condition/custody, any outgoing message and its delivery status, and the precise promise to the Baleiners. End on that changed position. The party earns an actual escape or chosen stay, without a mandatory final ambush.

## Eventualities - combat and resources

- **Players de-escalate:** Resolve offers on their merits even after initiative. Give opponents real reasons to stop: a clear path out, surrender, an apology plus restitution, or an agreement to testify.
- **Players flee:** State exits and obstacles. Enemies pursue only for their stated objective; beasts and trees stop at their territory. Use movement during initiative until separation is credible, then resolve the escape without requiring every enemy to reach 0 HP.
- **A fight runs long:** Opponents seek their objective or retreat; do not quietly double damage or add reinforcements. A captain escaping is a changed world state, not a failed encounter.
- **Several combats already occurred:** Reduce a later encounter to its visibly present advance element or allow avoidance based on information earned. Do not secretly remove enemies after announcing them or make every modular beat another resource tax.
