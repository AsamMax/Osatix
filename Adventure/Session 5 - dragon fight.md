## Phase 1: Canopy Skirmisher

- **HP Pool:** 55 | **AC:** 15 | **Attacks:** Single Attack (Bite or Stinger)
- **Arena:** Dense jungle floor, heavy cover.
- **Tactics:** Hit-and-run guerrilla warfare. The drake uses camouflage to strike and instantly retreats into the foliage. It ignores difficult terrain.
- **Player Goal:** Ready actions, use crowd control, or destroy cover to pin it down.

---

## Phase 2: The Green Collapse

- **HP Pool:** 55 | **AC:** 14 (Injured) | **Attacks:** Multiattack (1 Bite + 1 Claw)
- **Trigger:** Phase 1 HP hits 0. Drake splits the canopy, causing massive logs to crash down.
- **Arena:** Slippery, elevated logs, turn cover into danger.
- **Tactics:** Drake refuses to slow down, but no more hiding.

---

## Phase 3 (Standard): The Predatory Plunge

- **HP Pool:** 55 | **AC:** 13 (Cornered) | **Attacks:** Multiattack (1 Bite + 1 Claw + 1 Stinger)
- **Trigger:** Phase 2 HP hits 0. Drake grabs a player and weight-slams through the weakened floor into the floor.
- **Arena:** Turtle God rises, slowly reaching verticality, trying to shake off the intruders.
- **Tactics:** Feral draconic frenzy. The drake uses multiattack (1 Bite + 1 Sting) and fights to the death.

---

## Phase 3 (Alternate): Neurotoxic Trance

**Trigger:** If 2 players drop to 0 HP, or the whole party falls below 25% HP. The drake spews a cloud of neurotoxin, putting the party into a trance. The drake becomes invisible and the players must destroy three illusionary "Nightmare Pods" to wake up.

**Trance Mechanics:**

- xd6 kniffel rules -> until they all roll 6's
- The real drake is invisible and speaks from the shadows
- **Escape Condition:** Players must destroy three 1-HP illusionary "Nightmare Pods."
- **The Comeback:** Destroying the pods shatters the trance. Downed players wake up at 1 HP. The real drake is fleeing, it's tail caught in the giant turtle god's mouth. The drake is now at 1 HP and will escape if not finished off.
