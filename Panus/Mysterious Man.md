# Mysterious Man

## Lore

### Façon Aussire 

He is the head of the [[Fortress of Magusine]], which makes him closely connected to the [[Opera Crépuscule]] and tied to the government of [[Court of Aecor]]. He has heard of the prophecy of impending doom and, unsatisfied with the apparent incompetence of the government, decided to prepare on his own.

He started working with multiple contacts, like the [[Consortium Baleinier]] to have important items first disappear, then be lauded for recovering them. In truth he commissions fakes to be created so he can regain both the true and fake item, keeping the former and returning the later to the owner. When interfacing with criminals he uses the Mantle to appear as if with black greying hair and pale red eyes. 

As such he has build up quite an arsenal of magical items giving him no small amount of magical power. Each of these gives of a magical glow of power and has to be recharged regularly at the source of magic hidden in the [[Fortress of Magusine]]. Without access or knowledge of this the items, if claimed by players, can only be used 5 times (or days) before fading.

In truth he has blond, fading hair and pale blue, near white eyes with a darker skin tone.

## Items

**Blade Ward Sigil.** A small gemstone with an inscribed sigil, worn at the center of a shield shaped pin that grants resistance against bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage dealt by weapon attacks.

**Resistance Ring.** A black ring with an inlaid white gem, that allows the user to roll a d4 and add the number rolled to one saving throw of its choice. 

**Guidance Ring.** A white ring with an inlaid black  gem, that allows the user to roll a d4 and add the number rolled to one ability check of its choice.

**Mantle of Illusions**. A black mantle with a hood made from dark silk with many small gem beads woven into the fabric. When activated the gems sync to create an illusion around the wearer. This can be the surroundings behind them to achieve something similar to invisibility or another persons form to achieve a disguise. The illusions can cover the whole body, as each gem can cast a minor illusion. When scrutinized with a **DC 15** **perception** check the illusions can be discovered as such but not entirely seen through. The illusions can only stay active for up to **10 minutes at a time**.

**Center of Gravity**. A marble stone surgically embedded into his sternum that allows him to change and split the direction of his gravity. As such he can choose to "fall" in any direction he wishes, gaining the ability to near fly. Unlike the other items this stone feeds of his innate magic power. Taking and mastering this power is possible, but very tricky.

## Traits

**Ear for Deceit.** Keen ear for picking out lies. Whenever you make a Wisdom (Insight) check to determine whether a creature is lying, treat a roll of 7 or lower on the d20 as an 8.

**Eye for Detail.** Bonus action to make a Wisdom (Perception) check to spot a hidden creature or object or to make an Intelligence (Investigation) check to uncover or decipher clues.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Two weapon attacks per turn, though only the first melee hit has the effects of the booming blade on it.

**Booming Blade**. *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 1d8 slashing +2 +1d8 thunder damage, and the target then becomes sheathed in booming energy until the start of your next turn. If the target willingly moves 5 feet or more before then, the target takes 2d8 thunder damage, and the spell ends.

**Second Attack.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, one target. *Hit:* 1d8 slashing +2

**Enhanced Fire Spitter.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, range 120ft, one target. *Hit:* 2d10 fire damage

**Sneak Attack.** 3d6 bonus dmg if advantage on the attack, or succeeded with Insightful Fighting

### Bonus Actions

**Insightful Fighting.** As a bonus action, you make a Wisdom (Insight) check against a creature you can see that isn't incapacitated, contested by the target's Charisma (Deception) check. If you succeed, you can use your Sneak Attack against that target even if you don't have advantage on the attack roll, but not if you have disadvantage on it.

This benefit lasts for 1 minute or until you successfully use this feature against a different target.

**Steady Aim.** Give yourself advantage on your next attack roll on the current turn. You can use this bonus action only if you haven't moved during this turn, and after you use the bonus action, your speed is 0 until the end of the current turn.

**Cunning Actions.**
- Disengage, Dash, Hide

## Reactions

**Uncanny Dodge.** When an attacker that you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to **halve the attack's damage** against you.

## Tactics

At first goes in close for a booming blade attack, then disengages and flies out of range to cause them to move. When targets learn not to move, changes tactics to ranged attacks, gaining advantage with insightful fighting and dashing out damage from a flying position with sneak attacked enhanced Fire Spitter attacks.

Will flee if HP fall below 20 as his life is of highest priority.
