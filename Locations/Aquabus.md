# Waterways

**Type:**  Location - Mode of Transportation
**Relevance:** Session X / Arc Y  
**Status:** Active / Planned / Completed

## Overview

The main civil hubs of the nation are connected by waterways, which all pass through the central city at the Waterway Hub in [[Court of Fontaine]]. Only state controlled vessels use these main lines, though roads and paths exist to get around outside on foot. However, as the main civil hubs are separated by oceans, the waterways remain the primary mode of transport for citizens.

The individual boats are piloted by [[Melusines]] who act like mascots or tour guides.
