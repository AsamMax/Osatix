## Overview

Located in the north the institute has only recently been reduced to rubble and replaced by an impromptu headquarters within an old coliseum.

Why? Well the institute exploded recently in a scientific experiment on new energy sources, or so the juniors were told. Only few of those actually in the know survived and getting questioning them on the classified details is, with the need to know basis of the hierarchy, against the law. Still, the experiment left behind giant, floating bodies of water and who knows what magical corruption (that may be spreading).
